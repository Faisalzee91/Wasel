Our Story

Last-mile, reimagined.

Wasel was born out of frustration with the status quo — bulky courier companies with rigid schedules and zero visibility. We saw riders on every corner and people who needed things moved fast, and we connected them.

Today, Wasel powers thousands of deliveries daily across Riyadh — for individuals, small businesses, and everyone in between.
